EngineLink 0.12.2: a transmit power the router can hear.

- The 0.12.1 report showed WPA2 working, but the link poor in one direction: EngineLink heard the router strongly (-45 dBm) while the router did not answer EngineLink's login frames (codes 2 and 15, about once a second), getting an address took 29 seconds and the link dropped. Frames from EngineLink were being lost. Full transmit power can distort on some ESP32-S3 boards or pull the supply down.
- EngineLink now starts on Medium (15 dBm) instead of Maximum. After 12 unanswered logins in a row it lowers its power one step by itself (11, then 8.5 dBm) and keeps the level that works. Diagnostics → Wi-Fi → Transmit power still lets you choose (Maximum, Medium, Low, Very low); the report shows the level and how often it was lowered.
- "GitHub answered -1" now reads "No internet: EngineLink is not on the router right now".
- Not yet tested on the board.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept. The transmit power starts on Medium once after this update.
