# EngineLink 0.17.5 — the own Wi-Fi picks the quietest channel

**Test version for the web installer. Bench-verified on the owner's board
(Standalone and router mode); not boat-verified.** Hardware: revision 1
board, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8R8. USB installation erases settings and internal
history; the Wi-Fi update file keeps them.

## Why

The slow own Wi-Fi of 0.17.3–0.17.4 was not the board. Two ESP32 modules
and three firmwares (EngineLink, a plain Arduino SoftAP and the owner's own
test sketch) were slow on channel 6 and fast on channel 1 at the same strong
signal: 0.45 → 5.58 Mbit/s, 73.8 → 13.4 ms. Channel 6 was busy with a
neighbour's traffic (plan section 19).

## Changed

- Before the own Wi-Fi starts, EngineLink listens about 1.5 s on channels
  1, 6 and 11 and measures how much air time other networks use there. It
  starts on the quietest. It keeps its previous channel unless another one
  is under half as busy and at least 5 points better, so it does not hop.
- While the own Wi-Fi runs and no phone is connected, it checks its channel
  for 1 s every 10 minutes; two busy checks in a row (10 % or more) make it
  measure again and move if a channel is clearly better. A connected phone
  is never moved.
- On by default. The last choice is remembered. `apch=0` in the settings
  API is automatic, `1`/`6`/`11` a fixed channel as before.
- The network report shows the channel survey (busy share per channel,
  surveys, checks, moves) while the own Wi-Fi is on.
- Router mode is unchanged: the router decides the channel.

## Measured on the board (2026-11-14)

- Standalone: the survey chose channel 1; 212 → 562 kB/s in the first 30 s
  (channel 6 the same day: 18–115 kB/s).
- Router mode, home router on channel 1: 26 minutes, 784 of 784 pings
  answered (average 2 ms), 1173 web requests without a failure, 427–617 kB/s
  at the end. USB and bench supply gave the same.

Not verified yet: a channel move during operation, and the boat.
