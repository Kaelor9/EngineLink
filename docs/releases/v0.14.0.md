# EngineLink 0.14.0 — gear and clutch, guided drive test, four readings a second

On a KAD300/DP the EDC shifts electrically and a cone clutch in the drive
couples the propeller. This version watches that chain.

- **Gear and clutch.** Every shift at idle is timed: from the gear bits (value 264) to the propeller taking load, and from neutral to the load going. The load is seen in the fuel rate: on the boat about 2.2 L/h in neutral against 3.0–3.3 L/h in gear at 600 rpm. New events: **Propeller still pulled in neutral** (2.5 s after the bits say neutral) and **Fast shift / shift above idle** (each one counted, listed at most every 10 minutes per engine). Engines tab: the gear now, whether the propeller pulls, shifts, lever-to-clutch and neutral-to-clutch-out times with the first ones as a reference, hard shifts and a small trend.
- **Guided drive test** (Engines › Gear and clutch, or Diagnostics › Calibration), at the dock or on open water. Safety steps first (lines, nobody in the water, how to stop, a lookout), each with a small, slow animation of what to do. At the dock one engine at a time, in gear only at idle; on open water also 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 rpm, an acceleration and the sync. It learns the gear bits (also when they only tell neutral from in gear) and the compressor bit, and measures each drive. It replaces "Learn the gear".
- **Faster gear bits.** While an engine runs below 1,300 rpm, 264 is read about every 0.7 s and the lever value about every 2 s. The other slow values then come about every 15 s and count as current for 20 s.
- **Four readings a second** of rpm, fuel, lever, boost, gear bits and propeller load: the last 10 minutes in memory, each trip on the SD card (`/TRIPS/Tnnnnnn.FST`, as CSV from `/api/export/fast.csv?ids=`) and a minute before and after every event. Shown with the event and in a new **report for the mechanic** (one event, or the engines with the gear story and the boat).
- **Side by side:** idle fuel in neutral, idle rpm, compressor on accelerations, boost, warm-up time to 60 °C, charge air, charging voltage and the lowest voltage on the trip.
- **My boat** (Settings › Boat): the known data for this installation, your own notes and serial numbers (kept on EngineLink only, never in the GitHub reports), and the last drive test.
- **Service:** drive oil, anodes, belts and drive bellows added as yearly reminders.
- **Mark this moment** on the Trip tab while an engine runs.
- **Engine report to GitHub:** the events of the trip with time, engine and values, and a gear and clutch section. Reading paused after the ignition went off is no longer reported as a cable fault.
- **Discover more values** asks for 256–447 (was 256–319). None of the PC tool's commands reads fault codes; EngineLink still only sends the read request `01 43 21`.

**Compatibility:** ESP32-S3 EngineLink revision 1. An update from 0.13.x keeps settings, trips, events, the life story and fuel data. A gear learned with "Learn the gear" is kept; run the drive test to learn the compressor bit and to time the drives. New files: `/shiftlife.bin` and `/shifts.bin` (LittleFS), `.FST` files on the SD card.

**Not tested on the boat yet:** the shift timing was checked against the two recorded trips of 2026-11-02, replayed on a PC (about 10 shifts on port and 8 on starboard in trip 2, no false events), and on simulated shifts in CI. It has not run on the engines with the faster gear readings yet. The meaning of bits 0x100 and 0x20 in 264 is a hypothesis until the drive test confirms it on the boat. This is alpha firmware.

The release workflow regenerates `firmware/EngineLink/ui.h` from `firmware/ui/app.html` before building. For local Arduino builds run `python3 tools/build_ui.py` first.
