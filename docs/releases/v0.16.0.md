# EngineLink 0.16.0 — new drive test and NMEA 2000

**Drive test, all new.** Step by step on the phone, one lever at a time:
- Pictures of the levers.
- Tachometers.
- Safety checks before the test starts and on the way. "Nobody in the water" comes first.
- Pause and stop at any time.

EngineLink still only sends read requests to the engines. It never shifts or changes the throttle.

**NMEA 2000 (listen only).** EngineLink reads these from the boat's instruments, when they are on the bus:
- position, speed over ground, course and heading
- depth, log and water temperature
- rudder, wind, battery and tanks
- time from the GPS, which sets the clock

The values show live in the app and on the trip page. They are also stored once a second beside each trip (`/TRIPS/Tnnnnnn.NAV`) and come along in the second-by-second export.

**Devices and data** (Diagnostics) lists every device on the bus: maker, every message it sends and how often. It now has names for AIS and Raymarine autopilot messages too.

**Record the bus.** Also under Devices and data. It records every raw NMEA 2000 message for 10 or 30 minutes to one file on the SD card (`/NMEA/BUS.CSV`, at most 24 MB). The next recording replaces it. Download it as CSV to find out everything the boat sends, including messages EngineLink does not decode yet.

EngineLink never sends anything on NMEA 2000.

Update from 0.15.1 keeps everything. Trips from before 0.16.0 keep their format; they just have no navigation columns. Not yet tested on the board.
