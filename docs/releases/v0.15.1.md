# EngineLink 0.15.1 — no more pauses of several seconds

The first 0.15.0 reports (#141–#143) showed Wi-Fi itself working well:
- The router connection was up in 2.5 s (1.4 s to join, 1.1 s for an address).
- Pings to the router came back in 1–3 ms with nothing lost.
- No disconnects.

The slowness came from the main loop standing still for up to 9.4 seconds.
During such a pause neither the app page nor engine reading got a turn. Two
causes are fixed:

- **USB serial.** With a computer on the USB port that does not read it (for
  example after the web installer), every status line waited up to 2 seconds.
  Output is now dropped instead when nobody reads it.
- **Daily clean-up of old raw logs on the SD card.** It ran in one go and took
  7.4 seconds. It now works a few files at a time, between other work.

Also:

- **Switching from EngineLink Wi-Fi to the router.** EngineLink now tells the
  phones that its own Wi-Fi is going away. They can then move to the router at
  once instead of waiting until they notice it is gone.

Update from 0.15.0 keeps everything. Not yet tested on the board.
