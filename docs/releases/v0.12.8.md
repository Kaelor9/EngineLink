EngineLink 0.12.8: frame bundling from the router off, tested by the board itself.

- The 0.12.7 report: pings to the router were sometimes 1 ms and sometimes 1.2 s (average 60-240 ms), and the phone downloaded at 4-30 kB/s. A link that can answer in 1 ms but often waits hundreds of ms points to frame bundling (A-MPDU) from the router: when one frame of a bundle is lost, the receiver holds the rest until it is resent or a timer runs out. In router mode EngineLink now asks the router for single frames.
- The board checks this itself: once (and whenever you tap "Test now" under Diagnostics → Wi-Fi) it pings the router with bundling on and with it off, keeps the faster one and shows both results in the network report. Each way needs a reconnect of about 2 seconds. "Frame bundling from the router" in Diagnostics sets it by hand.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept.
