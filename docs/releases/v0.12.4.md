EngineLink 0.12.4: finds the transmit power the router hears best.

- Report #66: the router link is now stable (login 0.4 s, address 1.6 s, no drops), but 32 KB took 1.9 s through the router against 0.05 s on EngineLink's own Wi-Fi, a sign that frames from EngineLink still need retries. Once after each start on the router (60 s after connecting), and when you tap "Test now" under Diagnostics → Wi-Fi, EngineLink pings its router with 20 large packets at each transmit power (19.5, 15, 11, 8.5 dBm) and keeps the step with the fewest losses and the shortest round trip. The results are shown in the app and in the network report. Engine reading and the web server carry on meanwhile.
- The signal bars in the router list are drawn as four bars on one baseline (the old text characters looked different on every phone).

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept.
