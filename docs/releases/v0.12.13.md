EngineLink 0.12.13: the speed test and live data answer at once, and the report names what crowds the router's channel.

- 0.12.12 report: pings to the router 34-60 ms, but a tiny request to the speed test took 1.9-3.4 s to answer. The speed test and live data servers now run on the second processor core at a priority above engine reading, so they answer as soon as a request arrives.
- The scan in the start-up test now lists every network on or next to the router's channel (channel, signal, maker prefix of its MAC address, whether it is the router's own guest/mesh network; no names) and which of channels 1, 6 and 11 is quietest around EngineLink. The report showed a network on the router's channel 1 at -35 dBm, stronger than the router itself.
- The Wi-Fi card points out a slow router link from the speed test too (under 40 kB/s or slow answers with a strong signal) and names the channel to set the router to.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept.
