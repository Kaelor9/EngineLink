# EngineLink 0.17.6 — router lost: the own Wi-Fi stays

**Test version for the web installer and the app's update. Built (ESP32-S3
compile, source checks); not run on the board, not boat-verified.** Hardware:
revision 1 board, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8R8. USB installation erases settings and
internal history; the Wi-Fi update keeps them.

## Why

On the boat (2026-11-14, 0.17.5, router mode with a phone hotspot as the
router) the app kept showing "Reconnecting". The Wi-Fi log on the SD card
shows why ([evidence](../test-evidence/boat-2026-11-14/README.md)):

- While the router was not found, EngineLink's own Wi-Fi was on. Every
  12 minutes it was switched off for 2 minutes to look for the router, if no
  phone was joined at that very moment. At home that evening: 33 such cycles
  in a row. On the boat the own Wi-Fi went off the second the last
  phone left it.
- The hotspot link itself lost 34–69 % of the pings (not changed by this
  version).

## Changed

- **Router not reached: EngineLink goes to its own Wi-Fi and stays there
  while a phone uses it.** It tries the router again only after 10 minutes
  without any phone joining or leaving, and then for 20 seconds, not
  2 minutes. Before: every 10 minutes if no phone was joined at that moment,
  for 2 minutes.
- A lost router connection brings the own Wi-Fi after 30 seconds (the
  driver's own reconnects come first) instead of 2 minutes. After a start
  the router still gets 2 minutes: a boat router may start slower than
  EngineLink.
- The app says why and since when: "Router connection lost at 15:43 ·
  EngineLink is on its own Wi-Fi", on the Boat router page and in
  Diagnostics, with a **Try the router now** button. The saved choice stays
  Router: every start tries the router first.
- **The Wi-Fi log and the saved Wi-Fi reports can be downloaded in the app**
  (Settings › Diagnostics › Wi-Fi › Advanced), also the older, rotated
  files. Before, they could only be read over USB.
- Sea temperature: the value 273.05 K (−0.10 °C) in PGN 130311 is what a
  Raymarine SeaTalk1 converter sends without a temperature sensor (the same
  value for a whole trip on the owner's boat). It is no longer shown or
  logged as a reading. Limit: a real sensor reading exactly −0.10 °C is
  hidden for as long as it stays exactly there.

## Not changed

- Standalone mode, the station's radio settings and the automatic channel of
  0.17.5.
- The drive test still asks for live data while it records: its safety
  guards (rpm at the dock, boat moving, connection lost) read it.
