EngineLink 0.12.12: transmit power is fully automatic.

- EngineLink tests its transmit power by itself: 60 seconds after joining the router and again every 30 minutes while nobody is downloading. It keeps the strength the router answers best and only changes when another one is clearly (20 %) better, so a busy channel does not make it jump around.
- "Test speed" is a single quick test again (64 KB); it no longer tries the strengths itself. There is nothing to set or press for the transmit power.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept.
