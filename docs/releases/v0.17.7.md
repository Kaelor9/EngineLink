# EngineLink 0.17.7 — every trip keeps its own Wi-Fi reports

**Test version for the web installer and the app's update. Built (ESP32-S3
compile, source checks); not run on the board, not boat-verified.** Hardware:
revision 1 board, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8R8. USB installation erases settings and
internal history; the Wi-Fi update keeps them.

## Why

The Wi-Fi reports of the boat trip of 2026-11-14 were needed the next day to
see whether EngineLink answered slowly while both engines ran. They were
gone: `/WIFIREP.TXT` and `/WIFIREP.OLD` hold 256 KB each, about 13 hours of
reports together, and the board had stood on all night. 0.17.6's notes said
the trip's reports were still on the card; that was wrong.

## Changed

- **While a trip runs, EngineLink saves its Wi-Fi report every 10 minutes
  and once more when the trip ends**, in `/TRIPS/Tnnnnnn.WIF` beside the
  trip's other files. It is kept as long as the trip (until the card is
  wiped), at most 1 MB per trip. The router's name is hidden, as in the
  other saved reports.
- In the app: History › a trip › Export › **Wi-Fi during this trip**
  (`GET /api/wifi/log?f=4&id=<trip>`). Trips recorded before 0.17.7 have no
  such file.

## Not changed

Everything else is 0.17.6. `/WIFI.LOG`, `/WIFIREP.TXT` and their `.OLD`
files work as before.

## 0.17.6 on the board (2026-11-15)

Installed with the app's update on the boat's LTE router: joined at −46 dBm,
`/api/live` in 26 ms from a PC on the router by cable. The new downloads
worked: Wi-Fi log 37 KB, Wi-Fi reports 162 KB at 282 kB/s, older reports
263 KB at 274 kB/s, and "not on the card" for the older log that does not
exist. Not exercised yet: the router lost with a phone on the own Wi-Fi.
