# EngineLink 0.14.1 — a nicer drive test, and two small fixes

- **Drive test, new pictures.** One illustration style for every scene: the boat seen from above, flat and calm.
  - At the dock the picture changes between two ways of mooring. The lines go on one after the other, and the fenders are out.
    - Stern to the quay with the bow between piles.
    - Alongside, with bow line, stern line and two springs.
  - On open water the same boat lies on open sea with a soft wake.
  - "Nobody in the water" shows a swimmer by the drive and a red cross. Then the swimmer climbs the ladder onto the swim platform.
  - The scenes for "Know how to stop", "Engines at idle", "Someone keeps a lookout" and the lever are redrawn the same way.
- **Safety steps one at a time** instead of ticks. Each step shows its animation and is confirmed with "Done, next" before the next one comes, with "Back" to go back.
  - At the dock: nobody in the water, moored with the fenders out, how to stop, engines at idle.
  - On open water: open water, a lookout, nobody in the water, how to stop.
- **Discover more values**: with the ignition off the search now says it is paused and carries on by itself. The explanation says 256–447.
- **Export**: trips four times a second ("Four times a second", CSV) next to the second-by-second export.

Firmware unchanged apart from the version. Update from 0.14.0 keeps everything.
