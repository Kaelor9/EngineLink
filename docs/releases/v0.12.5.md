EngineLink 0.12.5: steady downloads through the router.

- Report #70: every transmit power got 20 of 20 pings back, yet downloads through the router ran at a few kB/s against full speed on EngineLink's own Wi-Fi. Single packets arrive and bursts stall: the router handles the ESP32's bundled frames (A-MPDU) badly. In router mode EngineLink now sends frames one by one. Diagnostics → Wi-Fi → "Frame bundling to the router" turns bundling back on if wanted.
- Wi-Fi power save is switched off again each time EngineLink gets its router address, and the network report shows power save, frame bundling and the negotiated Wi-Fi standard (802.11b/g/n).
- The firmware list in the app shows the 5 newest versions.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept.
