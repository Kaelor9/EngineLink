EngineLink 0.12.7: Auto transmit power from real download speed, and a far more detailed network report.

- Transmit power has six settings: Very low (8.5 dBm), Low (11), Moderate (13), Medium (15), High (19.5) and Auto (new default). On Auto, "Test download speed" in Diagnostics tries every strength with a real download to the phone and keeps the fastest. Until then the start-up ping test picks one. A fixed setting is never changed by the ping test.
- The network report now also has: ping min/avg/max for every strength, small pings to the router, pings to the internet (1.1.1.1, tells a slow router from a slow Wi-Fi link), every download test from the phone with its round trip, the router's Wi-Fi standards, 20/40 MHz, country and encryption (TKIP would cap the speed), how many other Wi-Fi networks share the router's channel, and chip, memory and main-loop timing.
- 0.12.6 report: pings 60-110 ms and 32 KB in 2.4 s through the router. This release collects what is needed to find the cause.

Settings, trips, the fuel log and fuel totals are kept. Transmit power starts on Auto.
