Try the EngineLink app

This is the real phone app from the EngineLink firmware, running in your browser against a simulated boat with two Volvo Penta KAD300 engines at cruise. Use it freely, or tap a step to jump to it.

Demo only: nothing here talks to a boat, and saving changes nothing. On a real EngineLink the app opens at http://192.168.4.1 on the board's own Wi-Fi.
